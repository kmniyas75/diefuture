1. Scope & Nature of Services

DieFuture Apartments assists international students, exchange scholars, and young working professionals in searching, identifying, and applying for rental accommodations (WG shared rooms, private studio apartments, and student housing) across major university cities in the Federal Republic of Germany (including Berlin, Munich, Frankfurt, Hamburg, Cologne, Aachen, Stuttgart, and Düsseldorf).

Our services include:

  • Curating matching listings that fit your specified budget, timeline, and city preferences.
  • Preparing professional German application dossiers (including German Anschreiben / motivational cover letters tailored for German landlords and WG flatmates).
  • Rapid, multi-portal application dispatch across 10+ German housing platforms (WG-Gesucht, ImmobilienScout24, Immowelt, Kleinanzeigen, HousingAnywhere, Wunderflats, Spotahome, and partner landlord networks).
  • Coordinating and scheduling virtual or in-person apartment viewings (*Besichtigungen*).
  • Verifying that room offers permit mandatory official City Registration (Anmeldung / Wohnungsgeberbestätigung).
  • Providing anti-scam contract checks and Kaution escrow advisory.

2.2 Direct Room Leads & Variable Fee Model

For seekers who do not require a comprehensive multi-portal package but wish to access individual, pre-vetted room listings or direct landlord connections, DieFuture Apartments provides individual verified leads under a Variable Fee Model:

  • Nature of the Lead: A "Lead" consists of a verified, currently vacant accommodation opportunity (WG shared flat, studio, or student apartment) that is confirmed to be Anmeldung-eligible with direct landlord/flatmate contact coordinates or pre-arranged viewing access.
  • Variable Fee Calculation: The fee charged for each lead is variable and calculated based on objective market parameters:
    • The target German city and housing market competitiveness (e.g., Munich, Berlin, Frankfurt vs. smaller university towns).
    • Accommodation category (single WG room, private studio apartment, or 2-room flat).
    • Immediacy of move-in date and seasonal demand cycles (e.g., September/October Wintersemester rush).
    • Bespoke landlord requirements and degree of direct intermediary negotiation required.
  • Mutual Agreement Prior to Payment: The exact variable fee payable for any specific lead is explicitly communicated in writing to the seeker (via WhatsApp or email) and must be approved by the seeker prior to lead release or payment collection.
  • Lead Verification Standard: Every lead provided under this model undergoes primary screening to ensure the listing is genuine, active, scam-free, and legally compliant with German tenancy registration laws.
Critical Clarification Regarding Rent & Security Deposits:
All fees paid to Stayforall Plus Services Private Limited (whether flat package fees or variable lead fees) are solely for accommodation search, lead matching, application dispatch, and relocation advisory services. You remain directly and solely responsible for paying your monthly rent (Warmmiete / Kaltmiete) and statutory rental damage deposit (Mietkaution, typically 2–3 months cold rent) directly to your German landlord or property manager pursuant to your private German tenancy agreement (Mietvertrag). We never collect rent or rental deposits on behalf of landlords.

3. Client Responsibilities & Accurate Information

To enable prompt application dispatch and avoid landlord rejections, the student agrees to:

  • Provide accurate, genuine personal details, educational enrollment proof (University admission letter / Immatrikulationsbescheinigung), and financial capability proof (Blocked Account / Sperrkonto confirmation or financial declaration).
  • Promptly notify DieFuture of any changes in target move-in dates, budget ranges, or city preferences.
  • Respond in a timely manner (within 12 hours) when our team forwards viewing interview slots or landlord invitations.
  • Maintain respectful communication with potential German landlords and flatmates during interviews and viewings.
