Questions & Answers

Frequently asked questions about Direct Room Leads

What does the ₹12,999 lead fee cover?

It covers our scouting fee, personal landlord liaison, verification of legal Anmeldung status, reservation of your exclusive viewing appointment, and tenancy contract audit.

How and when do I pay the room rent?

The monthly rent (€775) and deposit (€1,500) are paid directly to the landlord in Germany after you inspect the room and execute the German tenancy contract (Mietvertrag). DieFuture never collects rent or damage deposits.

What if I cannot attend an in-person viewing?

The landlord accommodates live interactive WhatsApp video calls for international students who are still in India or outside Munich, showing the bedroom, bathroom, kitchen, and balcony in real time.

Is the ₹12,999 lead fee refundable?

Yes. If the landlord chooses another applicant prior to your scheduled viewing or if the room becomes unavailable through no fault of your own, we either issue a 100% money-back refund or reassign you to an equivalent pre-vetted room without extra charge.
