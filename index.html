We apply across WG-Gesucht, ImmobilienScout24, Immowelt, Kleinanzeigen, HousingAnywhere, Wunderflats, Spotahome, Uniplaces, Studenten-WG, local university Studierendenwerk boards, and our private network of German landlords.
03 What does 24*7 response mean? +
In Germany, room listings are filled rapidly. Our bots and relocation team scan portals day and night, applying within 60 seconds of new listings appearing. With our 24/7 VIP package, you have a dedicated case manager reachable anytime on WhatsApp.
04 Are the rooms eligible for City Registration (Anmeldung)? +
Yes. Every single room offer we coordinate includes the mandatory landlord confirmation (Wohnungsgeberbestätigung) required for your German visa, bank account, and health insurance.
05 Can I apply while still in my home country? +
Yes. Over 70% of our clients are international students and expats who arrange their housing before arriving in Germany. We coordinate virtual viewings and verify lease legitimacy to protect you from rental scams.
